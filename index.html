OFFMAPPER-DRIVE-N8N

A private automation tool for the OFFMAPPER YouTube channel.

What it does

OFFMAPPER-DRIVE-N8N connects a self-hosted n8n automation, running on the owner's own computer, to the owner's Google account. It is used to produce documentary videos for the OFFMAPPER YouTube channel:

  • it reads and updates the owner's own Google Sheets that hold production tables (facts, scripts, voice and shot lists);
  • it saves and reads the owner's own production files (images, audio, video) in the owner's Google Drive.

The tool is used only by its owner. It is not offered to other users.
