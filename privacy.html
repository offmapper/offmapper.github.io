Privacy Policy

OFFMAPPER-DRIVE-N8N · Last updated: November 8, 2026

Who uses this app

OFFMAPPER-DRIVE-N8N is a private automation used only by its owner to produce videos for the OFFMAPPER YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own Google Drive files and Google Sheets that are used for video production: production tables (facts, scripts, voice and shot lists) and media files (images, audio, video).

How the data is used

  • To read and update the owner's production tables.
  • To store and read the owner's production media files.
  • At the owner's direction, text written by the owner in these tables (for example script text) may be sent to AI service providers, such as text-to-speech and language model services, to produce narration and video. No other Google user data is sent to them.

Storage and sharing

Data stays in the owner's Google account and on the owner's own computer, where the n8n automation runs. The app does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
